# Sarah Hsu - GreenOps for incident management: better recovery, lower carbon

[Synopsis](https://leanagile.scot/programme/greenops-incident-management-better-recovery-lower-carbon)

Key point: designing for the _required_ resilience means designing for lower carbon.

Repurpose existing metrics to highlight where infrastructure is under utilised. Use this to inform conversations about required capacity and resilience, and then refactor for actually required capacity and resilience.

## Things to try

1. Explore how our users understand and require resilience and availability. Determine how this could be expressed in a service defintion.
2. When we migrate our first workload, measure the before and after:
   1. Allocated capacity / resilience.
   2. Used capacity.
   3. Running costs.
3. Explore if I / EE should join the Green Software Foundation.
4. Read Building Green Software, by Sarah Hsu.
