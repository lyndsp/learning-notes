# What I learned about product and people at Agile on the Beach 2026

What connects BBQs, beaches, diversity, and digital? It's Cornwall's standout tech conference: Agile on the Beach (AoTB) [Link to LI company page].

This year I soaked up all this goodness in the wonderful company of several Equal Experts colleagues and clients, who attended as part of our Customer Network.

I'm currently the Technical Product Owner for a platform team, so the product and teams tracks were especially relevant. My key takeaways came from talks by:

- Ceri Newton-Sargunar, on being status-informed
- Rachel Dubois, on applying product-market fit to internal products, plus 10 lessons from her personal product mistakes
- Kal's lightning talk, "What is service design?"

Ceri's talk was inspiring for a future team retrospective to surface worries and risks. Ask: what am I worried about, and what risks am I seeing? Then have the team group output into:

- Things they can change
- Things they can change with help (who, when, and how could help?)
- Things they can't change

Rachel's talks gave a wealth of practical, actionable advice:

- Explore user needs, personas (https://medium.com/john-lewis-software-engineering/building-for-our-users-data-driven-personas-to-shape-our-platform-f8670726b666), jobs to be done, current pains, joys, and motivations to switch internal products.
- Establish product-market fit so you build a product internal teams push to adopt, rather than one they have to be dragged onto.
- Follow lean product basics: define the problem, form a hypothesis, segment, experiment, and adjust. Don't build a whole new tool or platform and expect people to use it.
- Track how much your internal product costs to build and run, and determine ROI (easier said than done for internal products, though DevX metrics might help).
- Create frequent opportunities for the team to call out assumptions and what's going unsaid.

Kal's lightning talk, "What is service design?", reminded me that even for internal products we need to consider:

- What is the ecosystem?
- What are all the interactions, across human and non-human touchpoints?
- What is the end-to-end journey: onboarding, adjustment, daily interaction, and offboarding?
